# Brick Sorter

Photograph a jumbled LEGO pile and sort the pieces into your sets. The app finds each piece in the photo, identifies it with [Brickognize](https://brickognize.com), matches it against your sets' parts lists from [Rebrickable](https://rebrickable.com), lets you choose when a piece fits more than one set, and tracks how many pieces you've found per set.

Open it in Chrome on Android and choose **Install app** to put it on your home screen. Data stays on the phone (back it up from Settings).
